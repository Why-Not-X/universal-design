Who is Blaster?

Blaster is an Autobot from the original Generation 1 Transformers series. He transforms into a boombox, and he serves as the Autobots' communications officer, handling their radio signals and broadcasts.

He loves all Earth music, especially loud rock 'n' roll, and he is happy to share it with anyone, whether they want to hear it or not. That energy makes him a great friend in a fight, but his music also gets him into trouble with his commanders. Of the other Autobots, Jazz is one of the few who shares his taste in music.

Cassette partners

Blaster's cassette team rides in his chest and jumps out to help in battle.

  • Steeljaw: transforms into a lion.
  • Ramhorn: transforms into a rhinoceros.
  • Rewind: a small robot who joined Blaster on missions with Perceptor.
  • Eject: a small robot who saw Blaster as his mentor.
