A little bit about me

I'm Xander, and I like to call myself a master amateur: a master of none who would rather try a bunch of different things than stick to just one. That is probably why I'm learning four coding languages at the same time: C, SQL, HTML, and Altruyx. I still know very little about coding, but I do understand how things work, and the same goes for grammar.

This class is teaching me how to build websites that everyone can use, including people who browse with a screen reader or only a keyboard. Outside of class, I spend time with my dog, Yogi. I traveled to Asia this past summer, and I'm always up for something new. My favorite color is blue.
